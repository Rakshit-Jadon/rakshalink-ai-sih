Generative AI deepfakes simulate law enforcement environments. Psychologically coerce victims to transfer funds urgently. 9% of total losses.

⚡ UPI Collect Abuse

Attacker registers VPA with spoofed Payee Name (e.g. "Electricity Board") while routing funds to mule account. 6.32L cases in FY26 alone.

📱 Smishing & APK Delivery

SMS masquerading as utility or bank. Links trigger download of malicious APKs that exfiltrate OTPs and UPI PINs. Primary attack vector for Tier-2/3 cities.

🌐 Typosquatting & Homoglyph

Registers near-identical domains (e.g. sbi-secure-login.xyz). Uses Levenshtein distance ≤ 3 to bypass human visual inspection.

📷 Malicious QR Codes

Physical QR codes stickered over legitimate restaurant/merchant codes encode attacker UPI VPA. Intercept happens at payment intent layer before banking app launches.

🧠 Hybrid Ensemble ML Architecture
TFLite INT8 quantized models — Char-CNN + LightGBM parallel inference with weighted probability fusion

1 · Character-Level CNN (60% weight)

  • Vocabulary: 70-symbol mapping — lowercase a–z, digits 0–9, RFC URL special characters
  • Embedding Layer: 128-dimensional continuous character vector space
  • Sequence Constraint: Max 200 chars — zero-padded (short) / truncated (long)
  • Conv Layers: 1D Conv filters → Global Max Pooling → Fully Connected dense layers
  • Activation: ReLU hidden layers · Sigmoid output (binary phishing classification)
  • Advantage: Learns character substitution, unusual subdomains, path obfuscation autonomously

  • Lexical: URL length, host length, dot count, subdomain depth, query count, token ratios
